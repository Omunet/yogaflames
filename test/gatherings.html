A large class is a different craft from a circle of twelve. The voice has to carry, the pace has to include the person at the back, and the shamanic thread has to stay human when the room is big. Martina can be asked to bring that. Whether she says yes depends on the event.

Where this fits

The kinds of rooms that ask

A community day

A class inside a neighbourhood event. The host provides the floor, the time, and a person at the door.

A festival field

Outdoor practice, weather included. Sound, shade, and a start time that respects the rest of the programme.

A one-off workshop

Two or three hours on one theme: breath, a season, or how to begin again. Title agreed with the organiser.

A private celebration

A class for a group that already knows each other. Not a performance bolted onto a party unless that is truly what they want, and even then the practice comes first.

Named festivals are omitted on purpose. If Martina teaches at one, it will be added with the organiser’s name and the year.
