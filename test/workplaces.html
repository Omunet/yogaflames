Sessions

What can be brought into a workplace

A single hour

One visit: movement, breath, and a way to land. Useful before a launch, after a heavy month, or as a first meeting.

A weekly class

The same short practice, often enough that people stop treating it as a special event. Length [duration].

A longer programme

Several weeks with a thread — focus, recovery, or the way a team speaks. The outline is written after a conversation, not before.

Movement for desks

Hips, spines, and shoulders that have been in a chair. No gymnastics, no special clothes required beyond what people already wore to work.

Breath and attention

Short practices for the hour before a difficult meeting. Plain language. No one is asked to share anything personal.

A quieter close

If Martina offers sound or a longer rest, it will be listed as [if offered]. It is not assumed here.

How a visit runs

Chairs, a meeting room, or a clear floor. Group size [maximum places]. Shoes off only if the floor allows it.

Away-days

Why this teacher

A shamanic eye, and a practical hour

Martina’s approach notices the tone of a group and answers it with movement and breath rather than with a speech. She is a yoga teacher from Sicily, based in Barcelona. This page does not invent a corporate career for her.

Questions teams ask

Do people need to be fit, or dressed for yoga?

No. The work can happen in work clothes and on a chair. Anyone who wants a mat on the floor should have room to lie down.

Can it be online?

Yes, as a live session. A camera and a little floor space help. The tool is [video tool].

Is this confidential coaching?

No. It is a movement and breath session for a group. People are not asked to disclose private matters.
