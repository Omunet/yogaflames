Retreats

A few days with nothing else to be.

Time away from the city for movement, silence, shared meals, and the kind of attention that does not fit in an evening class. No dates are confirmed yet.

A retreat, in this house, is not a holiday with yoga attached as decoration. It is a small group, a simple rhythm, and enough hours that the nervous system believes you. Martina would rather run fewer of them, and mean them.

Place, season, and price below are waiting for her. Nothing is on sale through this page.
