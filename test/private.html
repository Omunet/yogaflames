Private sessions · Barcelona and online

A class that starts from your actual day.

One-to-one practice for when a group is the wrong size: recovery, a new beginning, a question you do not want to ask in public, or a stretch of time you want guarded.

Why private

The pace belongs to one nervous system

Group classes have a shared rhythm. A private hour can follow an injury, a pregnancy only if Martina confirms she offers that, a return after time away, or simply a wish to be taught without an audience. This page does not assume which of those she is taking. Ask.

A live hour on [video tool]. You will want enough floor to lie down, and a camera angle that shows your whole body if you want hands-off guidance. Dawn Practice, the open online class, is separate and listed on the week.

Adaptation

The hour bends

  • Beginners get fewer shapes and more time inside them.
  • Experienced students can study one thing in depth, including the shamanic thread.
  • If something hurts, the shape changes. Bravery is not the method.
  • Tell Martina about injuries, pregnancy, or dizziness before the first session, not during the hard part.

Yoga is a physical practice. If your health needs particular care, check with a clinician you trust before you start, and mention injuries when you write. Nothing on this site is medical advice.
